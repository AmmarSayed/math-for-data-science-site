Joint probability

The chance that both events happen.

When outcomes are equally likely, list the complete outcomes and divide the number that satisfy both conditions by the total number of outcomes.

When A and B are independent, knowing A happened leaves the probability of B the same. Multiply the two individual probabilities.

P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B) for independent events

Example. Two independent draws each have a 1/3 chance of selecting blue. The chance of selecting blue twice is 1/3 × 1/3 = 1/9.
