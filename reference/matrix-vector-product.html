Matrix–vector product

Entry i of Xw is row i of X dotted with w.

(m×n matrix) × (n×1 vector) = (m×1 vector)

The inner sizes must match: each row of X needs one entry of w per column. The outer size gives one result per row. If the inner sizes differ, the product is undefined.

Example. X has rows (2, 1), (4, 3) and (1, 5), and w = (3, 2).
Row 1: 2·3 + 1·2 = 8 · Row 2: 4·3 + 3·2 = 18 · Row 3: 1·3 + 5·2 = 13 · Xw = (8, 18, 13)

Reading it. If rows are cases (customers, houses, records) and w holds weights (prices, coefficients), Xw gives one weighted total per case. Linear regression predictions are this product.
