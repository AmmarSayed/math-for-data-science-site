Mean and median

Two measures of center answer different questions about the same data.

Mean. Add the values and divide by how many there are. If one value increases by 10 in a set of five, the mean rises by 10 ÷ 5 = 2.

Median. Sort the values. With an odd count, take the middle value. With an even count, average the two middle values. An extreme value can change while leaving those middle positions intact.

Example. For 2, 3, 3, 4, and 18, the mean is 30 ÷ 5 = 6 and the median is 3. The mean is the equal share of the total. The median is the middle of the sorted list.
