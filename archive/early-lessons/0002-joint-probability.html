What are the chances of both?

Find the probability that two independent events both happen. In data analysis, that can mean estimating how often a record passes two separate checks.

Count complete outcomes

In the diagnostic you had two flips. Each cell shows a full result for both flips. Count one match out of four possible results, so the chance is 1/4.

You can also take half of a half. Half of all pairs start with heads. Of those, half also end with heads because the second flip has the same chance either way. That is what independent means here.

P(heads on both) = 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/4

For two independent events A and B, multiply their probabilities to find the chance that both happen. The individual chances can be different.
