Slope as a signed rate

Find how much an output changes for each extra unit of input. The same idea helps you read a regression coefficient or interpret a derivative.

Trace the change

Use the same right-triangle sketch you used for distance in the diagnostic. For slope, keep the direction of each change.

On y = 3 − 2x, when x goes from 0 to 2, y goes from 3 to −1. Subtract start from finish in both coordinates.

Slope = change in y ÷ change in x
(−1 − 3) ÷ (2 − 0) = −4 ÷ 2 = −2

For every 1 unit to the right, y falls by 2 units. That explains the coefficient −2 in the equation.

Find the rate

1. A line passes through (1, 8) and (5, 2). What is its slope?

2. A queue falls from 50 tickets at hour 2 to 35 tickets at hour 7. What is its average change in tickets per hour?
