Conditional probability

If you already know which group a case belongs to, calculate the rate within that group. This is how you compare completion rates, error rates, or other outcomes across groups.

Choose the denominator first

In your diagnostic, you found the 30 women who completed the course. The question said given that the person is a woman, so the group to divide by was the 60 women. Try the same move with a table.

Pick one of the 100 learners at random. If you learn they took the course online, restrict your count to the online row. There are 40 possible learners left, and 24 of them completed it.

P(completed | online) = 24 ÷ 40 = 60%

The bar means given. The words after it tell you which group to use. If the question asks for the chance a learner was online and completed, all 100 learners are in play, so that probability is 24 ÷ 100 = 24%.
