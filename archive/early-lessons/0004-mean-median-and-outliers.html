Mean, median, and outliers

Change one extreme value and see what happens to each measure of center. Then choose a measure that describes a typical case.

Change one number

In your diagnostic, you noticed that the 18 could pull the center away from the cluster. That pull applies to the mean because it uses the sum of all the values.

Mean = total ÷ number of values
Before, 20 ÷ 5 = 4
After, 30 ÷ 5 = 6

The median uses position. Sort the values and find the middle. With five values, that’s the third one. It’s 3 before and after.

For a typical wait or delivery time, the median can better describe the cluster when a few cases take much longer. Use the mean when you want the total spread evenly across all cases.

Calculate and interpret

1. Five wait times are 3, 4, 5, 6, and 12 minutes. Replace 12 with 22. What are the new mean and median, in that order?

2. Five deliveries took 4, 5, 6, 7, and 38 days. Which measure and value better describe a typical delivery in this set?
